FAQ

Is local radar and textual forecasts available outside the US?

Local NEXRAD radar and National Weather Service textual forecasts are US-only. Global radars and hurricane tracking work worldwide.

Does it support Dark Mode?

Yes. Menu bar conditions adapt to Dark Theme—enable in Preferences. The app follows system appearance throughout.

Can I use multiple locations?

Unlimited. Switch between them seamlessly for home, work, or travel. Supports Imperial/metric and 12/24-hour formats.

How current is the data?

Local weather updates every 15 minutes. Radars reload on demand for the latest. Projections and maps pull live sources.
